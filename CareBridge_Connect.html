Who we are

Mission and vision

Our mission

To connect individuals, families and communities in need with compassionate care, professional counselling, legal support, mediation and verified donors, through ethical, evidence-based social work practice.

Our vision

A caring society where no one faces hardship alone, and every person can access support, dignity and hope.

What we do

Ways we help

Care connection

We assess needs and link older persons, children, families and people living with illness or disability to trusted caregivers and services.

Counselling

Confidential individual, family, group and crisis counselling from trained social workers.

Donor matching

We connect donors, philanthropists and organisations to verified needs, and report openly on how gifts are used.

Legal support

Guidance and referral on legal matters relating to care, family welfare, rights of vulnerable persons, and access to justice.

Mediating services

Facilitated mediation to resolve family, community and care-related conflicts peacefully and fairly.

Our goals

Objectives

  1. Identify and assess vulnerable persons and link them to suitable care providers and services.
  2. Provide accessible, confidential and professional counselling for individuals, families and groups.
  3. Offer legal support and guidance on care, family welfare and the rights of vulnerable persons.
  4. Provide mediating services to resolve family, community and care-related conflicts peacefully.
  5. Mobilise and match donors, volunteers and partners to verified needs.
  6. Ensure transparency and accountability in all donations and referrals.
  7. Build partnerships with hospitals, faith bodies, schools, government and community groups.
  8. Promote awareness, advocacy and training on care, wellbeing and social justice.
  9. Follow up every case to measure outcomes and keep improving our services.
